Runbook — how the business runs

Where things live

  • Live site · GitHub repo (GitHub Pages, redeploys ~1 min after push)
  • Firebase — customer accounts
  • Firestore — carts & profiles data
  • Vedam Inquiries sheet — every contact-form submission lands here
  • Apps Script — deployment “Vedam Inquiries” feeds the sheet and this portal
  • Waitlist: the “Vedam Waitlist” sheet holds every signup; its own Apps Script deployment sends the confirmation, launch and campaign emails. Setup and operations: WAITLIST.md

How an order happens

  • Every piece is reservation, not instant purchase: a customer adds pieces to their cart (login required), then “Reserve Your Pieces” sends them to the contact form.
  • Their reservation arrives as a row in the Inquiries section above — reply by email to confirm fabric, measurements, timeline and payment.
  • Made-to-order pieces are non-returnable except for genuine defect or misdescription (see policies); one free fit alteration is included.

What happens on launch day

  • The moment the date passes, every page opens by itself — nothing to push or click.
  • Within 15 minutes the announcement goes to everyone on the waitlist, in batches, and each person is marked as sent — nobody is emailed twice, even if a send fails or the daily quota runs out partway.
  • Watch launch emails sent / pending in the tiles above. Pending clears itself; a failure can be re-sent from the ⋯ button on that person's row.
  • To open early, use Send launch announcement now. It cannot be undone.

Weekly owner checklist

  • Answer new inquiries — policy promises a 2-working-day acknowledgement and 1-month resolution for grievances.
  • Watch the Demand chart: pieces stuck in carts are your follow-up list and your restock signal.
  • Customers with a full cart but no inquiry row = warm leads worth a personal note.

Open items

  • Before the launch: deploy the waitlist Apps Script, put its /exec URL into launch-config.js, publish the ownerSecrets rule in Firestore, then set the launch date above. Steps in WAITLIST.md.
  • The site is closed to visitors until the launch date — every page sends them to the waitlist. Add ?preview=1 to any page to look around yourself.
  • Policies page still needs the legal entity name, registered address, phone number and named grievance officer (required by the E-Commerce Rules 2020) — only the email is published so far.
  • Policy copy should get a lawyer’s once-over before it is relied on.
